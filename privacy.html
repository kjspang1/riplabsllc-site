Privacy Policy

House Edge, by RIP Labs LLC, a Massachusetts limited liability company. Effective [DATE].

House Edge shows stock trades that members of the US Congress disclose publicly. We built it to need as little about you as possible: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or share your information for advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your watchlist (the members and tickers you follow), saved filters, My Committee and My Basket choices, and your appearance setting are stored only on your phone. We don't receive them unless you turn on alerts, below.

What we collect, and why

InformationWhenWhy
A random device ID and a secret key the app createsWhen you turn on alertsTo know which phone an alert belongs to. It is not tied to your name, Apple ID or advertising ID.
Your push notification tokenWhen you allow notifications for alertsTo deliver push alerts.
The members and tickers you turned the bell on for, your alert settings (trade size, buys or sells, big trades, other assets) and price alertsWhen you turn on alertsTo decide which new trades or prices to alert you about.
A record of which alerts were sent to your deviceWhen an alert is sentSo you never get the same alert twice, and to show Past alerts.
Your email addressOnly if you turn on email alerts and confirm it with a codeTo email your alerts. You can turn email off in the app or with the unsubscribe link in any email; "Remove this address" deletes it.
An anonymous purchase ID and your subscription statusIf you subscribe to House Edge ProTo unlock Pro features and check it before sending alerts. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or Apple ID.

We don't collect your name, contacts, location, photos, browsing history or any advertising identifier.

Services that handle this information for us

  • Supabase (database and email sign-in codes, hosted in the United States) stores the alert information above.
  • Expo (by 650 Industries) relays push notifications to Apple's push service.
  • Resend sends alert emails and email sign-in codes.
  • RevenueCat and Apple process and confirm subscriptions.
  • GitHub runs the scheduled jobs that load new filings and send alerts.

Each uses the information only to provide its service to us. When the app shows company logos (from Logo.dev) and member photos (from the public unitedstates.github.io project), your phone loads those images directly from those sites, which see your IP address like any website does.

Public data in the app

The trades, member names and photos in House Edge come from public disclosures filed with the Clerk of the US House of Representatives and the Secretary of the Senate, and from public sources about members of Congress. Stock prices are end-of-day closing prices from a licensed market data provider.

How long we keep it

We keep alert information while alerts are on for your device. Turning off every bell, removing your email address or deleting the app stops all alerts; you can ask us to delete everything tied to your device at any time (see Contact). Copies in our database provider's routine backups are replaced on their normal schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn alerts or email off in the app at any time, or turn off notifications in iPhone Settings.
  • Ask us what we hold about your device, or ask us to correct or delete it. Include the email address you used, or tell us you'd like help finding your device ID.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have these same rights. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those laws define it, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

House Edge is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us an email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Your device's secret key is stored only as a one-way hash, and the database lets the app reach only its own device's records.

Where it's available

House Edge is offered in the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll tell you in the app.
